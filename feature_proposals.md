# 🌌 FrethiX Future Feature Roadmap

Based on the current architecture (Next.js 15, Tailwind v4, Solidity Escrow), here are several high-impact features that can be added to enhance FrethiX (powered by Aethyl).

## 🌟 1. Reputation & Trust System
*   **On-Chain Ratings**: Allow clients and freelancers to rate each other (1–5 stars) and leave text reviews upon job completion.
*   **Verified Profiles**: Implement a "Verified" badge for users who have completed a certain number of jobs or verified their identity via worldcoin/Gitcoin Passport.
*   **Service Level Agreements (SLAs)**: Track on-chain metrics like "Response Time" and "Completion Rate".

## 💸 2. Financial & Payment Enhancements
*   **Stablecoin Support**: Allow payments in **USDC** or **USDT** to protect freelancers from ETH price volatility.
*   **Milestone Payments**: Instead of a single lump sum, break jobs into milestones (e.g., 30% on start, 40% mid-project, 30% on completion).
*   **Platform Fee**: Implement a small protocol fee (e.g., 1–3%) to fund future development, stored in a DAO treasury.
*   **Yield Escrow**: Automatically stake escrowed ETH in protocols like Lido to earn interest for the platform or the freelancer while the job is in progress.

## 🤝 3. Decentralized Dispute Resolution
*   **Arbitration Layer**: Introduce a "Juror" or "Arbitrator" role. If a dispute arises, a random set of trusted users can vote on the outcome, earning a small fee for their service.
*   **Evidence Submission**: Allow users to upload IPFS links to work evidence/chats during a dispute.

## 💬 4. Enhanced Communication & UX
*   **Web3 Messaging**: Integrate **XMTP** for end-to-end encrypted, wallet-to-wallet messaging directly in the app.
*   **Job Filtering & Search**: Add advanced search (by skill, budget, category) and filtering on the jobs page.
*   **Real-time Notifications**: Integrate **Push Protocol** to send browser/mobile notifications when a bid is received or a job is funded.

## 📂 5. Portfolio & Client Management
*   **Work Showcases**: A dedicated portfolio section where freelancers can link to GitHub, Behance, or on-chain proofs of work.
*   **Recurring Jobs**: Allow clients to re-hire the same freelancer easily for ongoing work.
*   **NFT Certificates**: Issue soulbound NFTs to freelancers who complete high-value projects as a permanent proof of skill.
